This app includes a subset of 30 MP3 samples, distributed by the Tone.js
project at https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/ . The app selects the
nearest recorded note and pitch-shifts intermediate notes with playbackRate.
It does not include the full instrument, velocity layers or release samples.
